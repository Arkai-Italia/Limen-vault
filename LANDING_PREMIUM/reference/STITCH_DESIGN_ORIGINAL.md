# LIMEN Vault — Landing Page Design System

## Brand Identity & Personality
- Brand: LIMEN Vault
- Tagline: Turn Your Documents into Searchable Knowledge — RAG Search & Automatic Wikis
- Aesthetic: Premium Light Editorial, ultra-clean Swiss typography rhythm, precision micro-borders, functional lime brand accents. Strict adherence to light slate surfaces (#FFFFFF, #F8FAFC, #F1F5F9), deep slate typography (#0F172A), crisp slate borders (#E2E8F0), and restrained fluorescent lime (#77F117 / #C8FF00) strictly for interactive badges, highlights, and status indicators.
- Mode: Light Mode only. No dark hero or dark sections. No gold, luxury textures, or heavy gradients.
